Rate Tips
How to Charge What You're Worth
Proven strategies from top freelancers worldwide
🎯
Anchor High, Negotiate Down
Always quote 20–30% higher than your target rate. Clients expect to negotiate. If they accept immediately, you quoted too low.
📊
Use Value-Based Pricing
Instead of hourly rates, charge based on the value you deliver. A website that generates ₹10L/month is worth ₹2L to build.
🌍
Target Western Clients
US/UK clients pay 3–5x more than Indian clients for the same work. Upwork, Toptal, and LinkedIn are your best platforms.
⏰
Raise Rates Annually
Raise your rates 15–20% every year. Inflation, experience, and skill growth justify increases. Existing clients get 3 months notice.
💼
Retainer Agreements
Monthly retainers (₹50K–₹2L/month) provide stable income. Offer a small discount (10%) for clients willing to commit monthly.
🚫
Never Justify Your Rate
When asked "why so expensive?" — pause, then say "This is my standard rate for this type of work." Confidence sells.
📝
Always Get 50% Upfront
Never start work without a deposit. 50% upfront protects you from non-payment and shows the client is serious.
🏆
Specialize to Command Premium
Generalists earn ₹1,000–2,000/hr. Specialists earn ₹4,000–10,000/hr. Pick one niche and become the best at it.
💬
Rate Negotiation Script
"My rate for this project is ₹X. I can start [date]. Would you like to proceed?" — Stop talking after quoting. Silence is powerful.
